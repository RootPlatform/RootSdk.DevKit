Enum indicating the reason for flagging content for moderation.

> **Copyright**: `3`

> **Dmca**: `2`

> **Harassment**: `7`

Harassment or bullying.

### Hatespeech

> **Hatespeech**: `5`

Hate speech or discriminatory content.

### Impersonation

> **Impersonation**: `10`

Impersonating another person or entity.

### Misinformation

> **Misinformation**: `9`

False or misleading information.

### Objectionable

> **Objectionable**: `11`

Generally objectionable content.

### Other

> **Other**: `1`

Other reason not covered by specific categories.

### Sexualcontent

> **Sexualcontent**: `8`

### Spam

> **Spam**: `4`

Spam or unsolicited content.

### Violence

> **Violence**: `6`

Violent or threatening content.
