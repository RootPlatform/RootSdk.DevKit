# Build a Root App

Welcome! Root Apps are full applications that live inside a community, with their own interface, server-side logic, and a private connection between the two. Whether you have a handy tool in mind, a game, or an idea nobody has tried yet, you're in the right place to bring it to life.

New around here? [Get started](get-started.md) walks you through setting up your machine and building your first App, one friendly step at a time. When you're ready for more, the guide below covers everything from your first idea to launch day.
