An enumeration that specifies the recurrence of a job in the job scheduler.

## Enumeration Members

### Daily

> **Daily**: `2`

Runs every day at the same time.

### Monthly

> **Monthly**: `4`

Runs once a month on the same day of the month.

### OneTime

> **OneTime**: `1`

Runs once at the specified start time.

### Unspecified

> **Unspecified**: `0`

### Weekly

> **Weekly**: `3`

Runs once a week at the same day and time.

### Yearly

> **Yearly**: `5`

Runs once a year on the same date (month and day).
