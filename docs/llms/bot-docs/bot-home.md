# Build a Root Bot

Welcome! Root Bots work quietly behind the scenes in a community, no interface required. They listen for messages, respond to events, help manage members, and take care of repetitive tasks so people don't have to. If there's something you'd love to automate, you're in the right place to make it happen.

New around here? [Get started](get-started.md) walks you through setting up your machine and building your first Bot, one friendly step at a time. When you're ready for more, the guide below covers everything from your first idea to launch day.
